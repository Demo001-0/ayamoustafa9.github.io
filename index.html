Does this sound like you?

Tap the one that sounds most like you. Even if everything’s fine.

  • What’s often behind it:It’s rarely missing information. Often it’s fear of choosing wrong, or too many voices in your head that aren’t yours.

    How I help:As you talk, I write down your exact words. Within one session, the pattern behind your hesitation shows up on the page, usually one specific fear. Once it has a name, you decide with your own voice instead of your fear. A wrong decision can cost you years. A clear one can start in an hour.

  • What’s often behind it:Many things are mixed together, so nothing looks like the first step.

    How I help:We lay everything out together and find which things are connected, and which one, once it moves, moves the rest. You leave with one clear first step, and if we keep working together, I follow up so it doesn’t fade by next week.

  • What’s often behind it:Sometimes you’re working hard on things that aren’t really your priorities, or giving yourself 90 tasks and finishing 3.

    How I help:I look at where your time and energy actually go, and what’s driving it. Sometimes it’s a fear of saying no. Sometimes it’s a quieter fear of what would change if you succeeded. Then we build a realistic plan, and I track it with you, session by session.

  • What’s often behind it:Usually missing boundaries, and a fear of upsetting people.

    How I help:We trace your “yes” back to what you’re afraid will happen if you say no. When you can name that fear precisely, you can set limits that fit you and your relationships, and I give you practical ways to keep them.

  • What’s often behind it:That feeling is real, even before it has words.

    How I help:This is the foundation of my work. Through questions, we move from “something’s wrong” to the exact sentence that describes it. That sentence is where real change starts, because you can’t fix what you can’t name.

  • What’s often behind it:Your business grew, but the way it’s organised didn’t.

    How I help:We find what’s really jamming your business: priorities, postponed decisions, or fears. Then I build you simple custom tools, so your business stops living in your head. Few coaches can also build the tool.

  • What’s often behind it:We’re often too close to our own lives to see them clearly.

    How I help:I notice patterns that are hard to see from inside your own life, and I ask the questions a friend usually won’t, because I have no agenda in your choices. One session often shows people something they’ve lived with for years without seeing.

  • What’s often behind it:Results and growth aren’t the same thing. You can achieve a lot and still feel stuck inside.

    How I help:We look at what you achieve and how you feel while achieving it, side by side. The gap between the two usually has a name. Once we find it, growth stops being something you chase and becomes something you feel.

If one of these felt familiar, you’re in the right place. You don’t need to arrive organised. Sorting it out is what we do together.

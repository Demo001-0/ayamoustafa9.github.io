What staying stuck really costs

There’s a voice that says: you shouldn’t need help. Do it alone, or the result won’t really be yours, and you won’t deserve it. Following it usually costs more: more time, more effort, and lost momentum. Asking for help doesn’t take away your credit. It just stops you from paying for every lesson the hard way.

And when life is fine, “fine” can quietly become a comfort zone you’re afraid to leave.

This isn’t for everyone, and that’s okay. It’s for people who are willing to look honestly at what’s holding them back. If you’d rather not look right now, I respect that. The door stays open.

Questions people ask

Is this therapy?
No. I help you see clearly what’s going on and decide your next step. I don’t diagnose or treat mental-health conditions. Maybe you need a therapist, a career coach, or someone who understands business. Most people don’t know, and that’s normal. With me, you find out what’s really going on, and if you need specialised support, I’ll tell you honestly and help you find it.
Are you a certified coach?
No. I don’t have a coaching certificate. My way of working comes from my own experience and from the people I’ve worked with. The best proof I can offer is their words, and a free call so you can see for yourself.
What happens in the free call?
We spend 30–45 minutes on what’s on your mind. I start with an empty page and fill it with your words as you talk. Patterns appear, we ask questions around them, and we end with a clear next step. You’ll see how I work, and decide if you want to continue.
Online or in person? In which language?
Arabic, English, or a mix, whatever feels natural to you. One-on-one sessions are online from anywhere, including outside Egypt, or in person in Alexandria by arrangement. The weekly group is in person in Alexandria.
Is what I share private?
Yes. What you share stays between us. The only exception is if I believe someone’s safety is at risk.

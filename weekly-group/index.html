فكّر قبل ما تقرر. A weekly space to think, in Alexandria.

What it’s for: learning to think clearly when you’re afraid, and to say exactly what you feel. Not “I’m worried” or “I don’t know.” The precise words that show you what’s holding you back.

You’re going through life. Working. Trying. Planning.
And inside, there may be a feeling you can’t quiet: “Something isn’t right inside me, and I can’t name it. Or I know the problem, but I don’t know how to deal with it.”

You need a place to actually sit with yourself. No noise. No one rushing you. No ready-made answers. That’s this space. It’s not a lecture. No one will tell you “follow your heart” or “everything will be fine.”

How it works: Every week has a topic. You sit, pick up a pen, listen to calm music, and write about it for 10–15 minutes. Then whoever wants to share, shares. Never your secrets or details: what you write stays yours. Just what you noticed about yourself while writing.

From what comes up, I ask questions and say the things people usually don’t say out loud, and together we trace it back to the fear underneath. One week the topic might be “comfort zone,” and the conversation reveals that comfort can be a hidden fear of success. Week by week, you gain the words to describe what you think and feel, and you start seeing your own patterns.

And there’s a moment that always comes: someone looks up with shining eyes. Not because someone gave them the answer. Because, for the first time, they saw it themselves. That’s the moment I built this space for.

A small thing I care about: I’ll meet you at the door. And the first time you come, there’s a sketchbook waiting for you. It’s yours. You bring it every week, write in it, and over time you can look back and see how far you’ve come.

The details:

  • Every Thursday, 6:30 to 8:00 pm
  • In Gleem, Alexandria. You’ll get the exact place after you sign up.
  • In a mix of Arabic and English
  • Up to 8 people, so it stays real
  • 250 EGP, so it’s within reach of the people who need it

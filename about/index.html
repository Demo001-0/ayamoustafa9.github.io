The crazy one who tried.

I’m Aya Moustafa, based in Alexandria, Egypt.

Even before I graduated, I knew a traditional job wasn’t for me. I tried several business ideas and failed, until I started writing CVs in college and sold more than 750 of them in a single year.

After graduating, I chose to build something of my own. Many people around me called it crazy and told me to play it safe. Under that pressure, I rushed. I made decisions out of fear instead of clarity, and I made a lot of mistakes.

Those mistakes taught me more than any book or course. I had to sit with myself and ask the right questions, and once I did, I started thinking completely differently.

That’s where فكّر قبل ما تقرر (Reflect Before You Decide) comes from. It’s the place I needed and couldn’t find: somewhere a person sits with you in the hard question, without rushing you to an answer. I hold it every week in Alexandria, and I share calm, educational content about it on Instagram. Many of the people I work with found me there.

I’d rather be the crazy one who tried than the sensible one who was afraid.

فكّر قبل ما تقرر also led me somewhere I didn’t expect. I wanted to help the people I work with so much that I went looking for new ways to do it. I noticed that a lot of what weighs on people, and on their businesses, doesn’t only live in their thinking. It lives in their days: scattered tasks, forgotten follow-ups, everything carried in their heads. So I started building tools with Claude, an AI tool: journals, exercises, and simple digital tools that turn clear thinking into something you can use every day.

I’m always looking for problems worth solving, starting with my own business. Whatever I build to help myself, I share here so it can help others too. We’re all here to help each other.

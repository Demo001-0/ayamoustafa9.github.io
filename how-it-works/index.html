  1. What they said (keywords):

    promotion · not ready · my father · everyone expects · tired

    As you talk, I write down the exact words you use. Not my interpretation, yours.

  2. Pattern:

    “Other people’s expectations” appears 4 times. “My own wish” appears once.

    The same words and themes start repeating. I notice them and ask about them. This is usually the moment people say, “I never saw it this way.”

  3. The question:

    Whose “ready” are you waiting for?

    I ask the questions that lead to what’s behind the problem, including the ones you’ve been avoiding.

  4. The precise sentence:

    “I’m not unsure about the job. I’m afraid of disappointing my father if I fail.”

    Once you can name something precisely, you can deal with it.

  5. Next step:

    Write down what you think ready looks like. Bring it to the next session.

    Every session ends with a next-step section: what to do, what to notice, what to bring next time.
